## Research Interests

I like math.
I like (most of) analysis.
My current research interests include functional analysis, numerical analysis, 
and numerical methods for differential equations.
During my bachelor's, I focused on numerical methods for production-destruction 
models.
My master's research centered on numerical methods for resistivity measurements
in axisymmetrical settings.

### Current Research
